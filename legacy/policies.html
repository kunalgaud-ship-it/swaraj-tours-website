Terms & Conditions

1. Booking and confirmation

  • A booking is confirmed only after our team shares the fare and vehicle details on WhatsApp or by phone and you accept them.
  • Enquiries sent through the website form are requests, not confirmed bookings, until we reply.
  • For outstation trips and tour packages an advance payment may be required to confirm the booking. The advance amount is told at the time of confirmation.

2. Fares and extra charges

  • Fares are quoted before the trip and are based on the trip type, vehicle, distance and duration you share with us.
  • Unless stated otherwise in the quote, toll charges, parking fees, state entry taxes, permit fees and night driving allowance are payable in addition to the fare.
  • Local packages (for example 8 hrs / 80 km) include the stated hours and kilometres. Extra hours and kilometres are charged at the rate mentioned in the quote.
  • Round trip and outstation fares are calculated from garage to garage unless the quote says otherwise.
  • Waiting time beyond the free waiting period mentioned in the quote may be charged.

3. Passenger responsibilities

  • Please be ready at the pickup point at the agreed time. Delays may affect the trip schedule and timing of later pickups.
  • Passengers are responsible for their own luggage and belongings. We are not liable for items left in the vehicle, but we will make every effort to return them.
  • Smoking, consumption of alcohol and carrying illegal or hazardous items in the vehicle are not permitted.
  • Any damage to the vehicle caused by a passenger will be charged to the passenger.
  • The number of passengers must not exceed the seating capacity of the booked vehicle.

4. Vehicle and driver

  • We make every effort to provide the vehicle model requested. If it is unavailable, a vehicle of the same or higher category will be provided.
  • Drivers follow road safety rules and may refuse unsafe instructions such as over-speeding or driving on closed roads.
  • The driver decides the route based on road conditions and safety, unless you request a specific route in advance.

5. Delays and events beyond our control

  • We are not responsible for delays caused by traffic, weather, road closures, strikes, accidents, vehicle breakdown or other events beyond our control. In case of breakdown we arrange an alternative vehicle as quickly as possible.
  • For airport transfers, please share your flight number so that we can track delays. Missed flights due to late arrival at the pickup point are not our responsibility.

6. Liability

  • Our liability for any booking is limited to the fare paid for that booking.
  • Vehicles are covered by the insurance required under Indian law. Passengers are advised to carry their own travel insurance for outstation trips.

7. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version on this page at the time of booking applies to your booking.

Privacy Policy

Information we collect

  • Booking details such as your name, mobile number, pickup and drop locations, travel dates, flight number and vehicle preference, shared by you on WhatsApp, phone or the website form.
  • Login details: if you use Customer Login, your mobile number is verified by a one-time password (OTP) sent through Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Website usage: our website does not use advertising cookies. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security.

How we use it

  • To confirm, manage and complete your booking, and to share trip and driver details with you.
  • To contact you about your booking, invoices and customer support.
  • To send offers and updates on WhatsApp only if you have agreed to receive them. You can opt out any time by replying "STOP".

Sharing

  • Your name, mobile number and trip details are shared with the assigned driver so that the trip can be completed.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We may share information if required by law or by a government authority.
  • The website form opens WhatsApp. Messages sent on WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy. OTP login is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Storage and security

  • Booking records are kept for accounting and legal requirements, and for resolving disputes.
  • We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your rights

You may ask us to view, correct or delete your personal information by writing to info@swarajtours.in or messaging us on WhatsApp. We will respond within a reasonable time, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

Cancellation Policy

To cancel a booking, message us on WhatsApp or call +91 98607 07287 with your booking details. Cancellation time is counted from the scheduled pickup time.

Local, one-way and airport transfers

  • More than 12 hours before pickup: free cancellation.
  • Between 4 and 12 hours before pickup: 25% of the fare or the advance paid, whichever is lower.
  • Less than 4 hours before pickup, or no-show: 50% of the fare or the advance paid, whichever is lower.

Round trips, outstation and tour packages

  • More than 48 hours before pickup: free cancellation.
  • Between 24 and 48 hours before pickup: 25% of the package fare or the advance paid, whichever is lower.
  • Less than 24 hours before pickup, or no-show: the advance paid is not refundable.

Other conditions

  • If a trip is shortened after it has started, the fare for the booked package or minimum distance remains payable.
  • If we are unable to provide a vehicle for a confirmed booking, you may cancel without any charge and any advance paid is refunded in full.
  • Peak season and festival bookings (for example Diwali, New Year, Kumbh Mela) may have separate cancellation terms, which will be told at the time of confirmation.

Refund Policy

  • Refunds are processed after deducting the applicable cancellation charge mentioned above.
  • Refunds are made to the same account or UPI ID from which the payment was received, within 7 working days of the cancellation request.
  • Cash advances are refunded by UPI or bank transfer. Please share your UPI ID or bank details on WhatsApp.
  • Any payment gateway or bank charges deducted on the original payment are not refundable.
  • If you believe you have been charged incorrectly, contact us within 7 days of the trip with your booking details and we will review it.
